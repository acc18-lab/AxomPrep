WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Contact AxomPrep Support

Need help using AxomPrep, found an error in a question or job update, or want to suggest a feature? Use the guidance below to send a clear support request.

AxomPrep supports learners preparing for ADRE, APSC, SSC and other competitive examinations with practice questions, quizzes, current affairs and study resources.

Website or account issue

Describe what happened, which page you were using, and the steps that led to the problem. If an error message appears, include its exact wording.

Report a content error

Tell us the page or question, the detail you believe is incorrect, and a reliable source or official notification where possible. For recruitment matters, always verify details on the official portal.

Premium or payment query

Include the plan name, approximate transaction date and payment reference if needed. Never send your password, OTP, card PIN or banking credentials.

Feedback and suggestions

Share the feature you would like to see, how it would help your preparation, and the exam or subject it relates to.

How to contact us

Please use the official contact method provided on the AxomPrep website or the support channel through which you accessed the service. We do not publish an unverified email address or phone number here.

For faster handling, use a subject such as “Login issue”, “Question correction”, “Job alert correction” or “Premium payment query”. Include only the information needed to investigate your request.

Before reporting a job notification

Include the job title, recruiting organisation, relevant date and link to the official notification. Application deadlines and eligibility can change, so rely on the official recruiting authority for final confirmation.
