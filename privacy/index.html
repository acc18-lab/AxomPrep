Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AxomPrep handles information when you visit or use our competitive-exam preparation website.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include details you provide when creating or managing an account, such as your email address; information you submit through support or feedback channels; and activity needed to operate learning features, maintain account security or troubleshoot errors. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide account and learning features, remember progress where supported, respond to support requests, process subscription access, protect the website from misuse, maintain service reliability and improve the platform.

3. Accounts and service providers

AxomPrep may rely on third-party service providers for website hosting, authentication, database services and payment processing. Those providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy practices. Payment details entered into a payment provider's checkout are handled by that provider; AxomPrep should not be sent your card PIN, OTP or banking password.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or its providers may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to keep features working, maintain sessions, improve security or remember preferences. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some features.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the platform, where you request a service that requires sharing, when required by applicable law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, users or legal rights.

6. Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, subject to operational needs and legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to verification, technical limits and any legal retention obligations. You can also stop using the website or manage relevant browser settings.

8. Children

AxomPrep is intended for people preparing for educational and competitive examinations, not for collecting personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information unnecessarily, contact us so we can review the request.

9. External links

Pages may link to third-party websites, official recruitment portals, book sellers or payment services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before sharing information.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and its services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the Contact Support page and clearly state that your message concerns privacy.

This page describes general website practices and is not legal advice. Review and adjust it to match the actual services, analytics, cookies and data flows enabled on the live website.
