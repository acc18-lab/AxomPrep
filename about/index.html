Our approach

We aim to present learning resources in a clear, mobile-friendly format. Exam notifications, dates, eligibility criteria and other official details can change; candidates should always read the relevant official notification before applying or making decisions.

Who is AxomPrep for?

AxomPrep is intended for students and job aspirants looking for accessible online practice and study resources for exams such as ADRE, APSC, SSC and other competitive examinations. The resources on the platform are for educational purposes and are not a guarantee of examination results or employment.
